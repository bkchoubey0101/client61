Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

When are you open?

We are open all seven days, Monday to Sunday, from 10:30 AM to 7:30 PM.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose your services on this site, fill in the date and time and submit. All details reach our WhatsApp and we confirm with you.

What is the Bridal Package price?

The Bridal Package is ₹14,999. Normal Bridal Look is ₹5,000 and HD Bridal Look is ₹8,000.

Can I get a custom service or look?

Yes! Use the Custom Request section and our team will reply on WhatsApp.

How much do straightening and keratin cost?

Normal or blow-dry straightening starts at ₹400; Permanent Smoothening and Keratin start at ₹4,000. The final price depends on hair length and type.
